Powerful Features for Everyone

Discover Trusted Providers

Search and browse verified service providers in your area using powerful filters for keywords (service type, price, availability, skills), location (county, sub-county), and categories. View rich profiles showing names, photos with story indicators, contact details (phone/email with copy buttons), service descriptions, key skills, areas served, pricing, availability, and last active time.

Instant Direct Messaging

Connect instantly with providers via the "Message" button and enjoy a full real-time chat with text, photo/video sharing, audio/video recording, emoji reactions, quoted replies, copy/delete options, and unread indicators. Access your centralized inbox for all conversations, receive notifications, and even message the platform admin directly for support.

Seamless Booking System

Book services effortlessly with the intuitive "Book" button — fill out a simple form with your details and preferred date/time using a calendar picker. Bookings are sent directly to providers in real time for quick confirmation, rescheduling, or responses.

Manage Your Bookings

Track all past and current bookings in your personal job history, view statuses (Confirmed, Rescheduled, Cancelled), updates, and details. Rebook favorite providers with one click, or delete old records to keep your history clean and organized.

Showcase Portfolios & Stories

Providers can upload portfolios of past work and post images/videos in a clean grid for customers to browse and enlarge. Temporary stories (photos/videos with captions) appear as colorful animated rings around profile pictures — tap to view full-screen with progress bars.

Reviews, Ratings & Trust

After a service, leave detailed feedback with overall star ratings (including half-stars) for quality, punctuality, and communication, plus required written comments. View average scores, all public reviews, dates, and provider replies to make confident choices.

Location & Maps Integration

See each provider’s service areas (county, sub-county, neighborhood) clearly marked on an interactive Google Maps embed. Get directions from your current location when geolocation is enabled, or simply view the pinned area for easy planning.

Enhanced Platform Experience

Switch between English and Swahili instantly, enjoy fully responsive mobile design with touch-friendly controls, and benefit from thoughtful UX features: copy buttons, smooth chat scrolling, loading spinners, success/error toasts, confirmation dialogs, dark-mode friendly purple theme, emoji picker, and a dedicated "Give Feedback" button for suggestions or bug reports.
