Welcome to FindService!

Your all-in-one platform to connect with nearby customers

📥
Inbox — Chat with Customers Use the Inbox button to send and receive real-time messages with your customers. Share updates, answer questions, and build trust through direct conversation.
📅
View Bookings — Manage Appointments Review incoming booking requests from customers. You can confirm, reschedule, or reject bookings — and always keep your schedule organised.
🔔
Notifications — Stay in the Loop The bell icon shows real-time alerts for new bookings, messages, and important updates. Never miss anything important from your customers.
✏️
Edit Profile — Keep Your Info Fresh Update your name, phone, service description, pricing, availability, and profile photo anytime so customers always see accurate information.
📤
Upload Projects — Showcase Your Work Upload portfolio items and list your key skills so customers can see exactly what you offer. A strong portfolio wins more bookings.
📸
Post Photos & Videos — Build Credibility Share photos and videos of your work. Visual proof of your skills helps customers feel confident and excited before they even book you.
⭐
Ratings & Reviews — Your Reputation View star ratings and written feedback from customers. Reply to reviews directly — showing professionalism builds loyalty and earns more clients.
💳
Payment Details — Get Paid Easily Add your M-Pesa or bank account so customers can pay you smoothly after service. You can add, edit or remove payment methods anytime.
✅
Activity Status — Control Your Visibility Use the green status selector to set yourself as Available so customers can find you, or Deactivate your account temporarily when you need a break.
📖
Booking History — Track Your Progress View a complete log of all past bookings — confirmed, rejected, and rescheduled — so you always have a clear record of your work history.
➕
Stories — Share Moments with Customers Post short-lived photo or video stories on your profile. A great way to share promotions, job updates, or behind-the-scenes glimpses of your work.
💬
Message Administrator — Get Support Need help? Use the Message Administrator button to reach our support team directly. We are here to help you succeed on the platform.

We are here to help you grow your business and connect with customers who need exactly what you offer. 💼 Fill in your profile completely for the best results!
