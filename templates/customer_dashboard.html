Welcome to FindService!

Your gateway to trusted local service providers

We're thrilled to have you here! FindService makes it effortless to discover, connect with, and book skilled professionals right in your neighbourhood — from home repairs to personal care and beyond. Here's a quick tour so you can hit the ground running:

📍
Providers Near You — First The top section always shows providers in your registered county and sub-county, so the most relevant help is always front and centre.
🔍
Powerful Search & Filters Use the search bar to find services by type, skill, price, or availability. Combine it with the County, Sub-County, and Service Category filters to zero in on exactly what you need.
💬
Chat Directly with Providers Tap Message on any provider card to open a real-time chat. Ask questions, share photos, or clarify details — all inside your secure inbox.
📅
Book in Seconds Press Book, fill in your name, phone, email and preferred date, and your request lands instantly with the provider. They'll confirm, reschedule, or respond via chat.
👁️
Explore Portfolios & Posts Before booking, browse a provider's past work through View Portfolio and View Posts — photos, videos, and project samples that speak louder than words.
⭐
Rate & Review After Service Your honest feedback builds the community. Rate overall quality, punctuality, and communication so future customers always know who the best providers are.
🔔
Stay in the Loop Your Notification bell and Inbox keep you updated on booking confirmations, reschedules, new messages, and more — in real time.
🗺️
Live Provider Location Tap View Provider's Location to get live, turn-by-turn directions straight to your provider — perfect for knowing exactly when they'll arrive.
📖
Job History & Quick Rebooking The Job History & Rebooking button lets you revisit past bookings and rebook your favourite providers in one tap — no refilling forms.
❤️
Save Your Favourites Add providers to your Favourites list so the ones you trust are always within easy reach for your next booking.
💡

Pro Tip: Provider cards also show a coloured ring when they have an active Story — tap the profile photo to peek at their latest updates, showcasing new services or completed projects!
